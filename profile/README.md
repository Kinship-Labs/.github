## About Kinship Labs

At Kinship Labs, we believe technology should make everyday life easier, not more complicated. We build useful, approachable software around real needs, with careful attention to the details that matter.

## Our Projects

### Gather

**Shared household organization · In development**

Gather is a shared grocery-list application designed to make household shopping simpler. With collaborative lists, offline functionality, and personalized item categories, Gather helps households stay organized and in sync.

Our first application is currently in private testing, with more projects planned for the future.
